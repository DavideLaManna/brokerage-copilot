### Notes for next developer:

- To use the Tradier adapter:
  1. Store credentials: `secretManager.setCredentials({ brokerType: 'tradier', oauth: { accessToken: '...' }, accountId: '...' })`
  2. Connect: `const result = await connectionService.connect('tradier')`
  3. Get adapter: `const adapter = connectionService.getAdapter('tradier')`
  4. Fetch data: `const positions = await adapter.getPositions()`
